Workflow Audit and Cleanup Plan

Example of a client-facing workflow review for a small service business dealing with inconsistent onboarding, unclear handoffs, and too much manual follow-up.

Service Type Workflow Audit
Typical Turnaround 2 to 3 Days
Ideal Buyer Small Service Business

Project Context

This sample is based on a common operating problem: new client onboarding is happening across email, memory, and scattered notes, which leads to missed steps, uneven communication, and unnecessary rework.

Observed Friction

  • Intake details are being captured in multiple places.
  • Task ownership changes between people without clear handoffs.
  • Follow-up timing varies depending on who is handling the account.
  • Client communication is inconsistent during onboarding.
  • Key onboarding knowledge lives in the owner’s head.

Current-State Workflow

  1. Client signs agreement.
  2. Internal team is notified by email.
  3. Setup steps begin manually across different team members.
  4. Documents and credentials are gathered inconsistently.
  5. Status is updated informally.
  6. Service launches after several manual checks.

Recommended Improvements

  • Create one standardized onboarding checklist.
  • Define a single owner for each onboarding stage.
  • Move all intake details into one shared record.
  • Use a consistent client welcome and follow-up sequence.
  • Add a documented exception path for missing documents or delays.

What the Client Receives

  • Current-state workflow summary
  • Bottleneck and risk review
  • Quick-win recommendations
  • Future-state workflow outline
  • Priority action plan

Priority Action Plan

Week 1

  • Build the onboarding checklist
  • Define stage ownership
  • Standardize intake fields

Week 2

  • Draft the communication sequence
  • Create a handoff rule to active service
  • Document the exception path

Week 3

  • Test the revised process on live onboardings
  • Refine based on friction points
  • Publish the final SOP

A strong workflow audit should reduce confusion immediately, not just produce a long list of observations.
