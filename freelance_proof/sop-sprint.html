Sample SOP Title

New Client Intake and Setup SOP

Purpose

Ensure every new client is onboarded consistently, completely, and with clear internal ownership from intake through service launch.

Process Steps

  1. Review the signed agreement and confirm service scope.
  2. Create the client record in the shared tracker.
  3. Log all required setup details in standard intake fields.
  4. Verify any missing documents, credentials, or approvals.
  5. Send the standard welcome message.
  6. Assign setup tasks to the correct internal owner.
  7. Complete required account, process, or file setup steps.
  8. Confirm completion against the onboarding checklist.
  9. Send status update or launch confirmation.
  10. Transfer ownership to the ongoing service owner.

Exceptions and Escalation

  • If required information is missing, pause setup and send a missing-items request.
  • If scope is unclear, escalate for manager review before proceeding.
  • If the client has urgent timing needs, flag the account for priority handling.

What the Client Receives

  • Structured SOP documents
  • Defined ownership and handoff notes
  • Formatting that is easy to train on
  • Process gaps called out where relevant
  • Reusable documentation template

Quality Checks

  • All required intake fields completed
  • Documents stored in the correct location
  • Welcome communication sent
  • Checklist fully completed
  • Final handoff recorded

Good SOPs do more than document tasks. They make training easier, reduce ambiguity, and improve consistency across the team.
